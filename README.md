## What it does

Skills Gap Analyzer builds a live skills inventory from resumes, project history, and self-assessments, then maps it against your strategic needs — showing exactly which skills to hire, build, or rent.

## Key features

- **Live skills inventory** — auto-inferred from work artifacts and profiles.
- **Strategy mapping** — skills demand modeled from your roadmap.
- **Hire/build/rent guidance** — per gap, with cost comparison.
- **Team heatmaps** — strengths and gaps visualized per team and location.
- **Succession signals** — bench strength per critical role.

## Who it's for

HR leaders, CTOs, and workforce planners aligning talent to a fast-moving roadmap.
